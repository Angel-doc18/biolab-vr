How a lesson is made

From the syllabus to your phone

Every lesson goes through the same four steps before a student sees it.

  1. Start from the syllabus

    Each topic begins with the MINESEC programme of the class and the scheme of work schools follow term by term.

  2. Write it to be understood

    Plain explanations, worked examples, exam tips, and at least five questions after every lesson.

  3. Draw what the exam asks

    Diagrams are labelled the way the GCE expects, with ruled label lines that never cross.

  4. Check before it is published

    Lessons are checked against the syllabus, and answers worked by the tutor are checked a second time.
