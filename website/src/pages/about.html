Why we made Klass8

Classes are large, textbooks are expensive, and extra lessons cost more than many families can pay. Yet most students already have a phone in the house. Klass8 puts the whole syllabus of their class on that phone, explained out loud, for {{PRICE_YEAR}} a year.

Every lesson is written for the MINESEC syllabus and checked against the schemes of work used in schools, so what students learn in Klass8 is what their teachers teach in class.
