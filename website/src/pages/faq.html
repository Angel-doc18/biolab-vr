Using Klass8

Is Klass8 free?

Your first month is free, with everything open. After that, Klass8 costs {{PRICE_MONTH}} for a month or {{PRICE_YEAR}} for a year. Nothing renews automatically.

How do I pay?

In the app, choose a plan, then MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money, and enter your number. Approve the request on your phone with your PIN. Payments are processed by Fapshi, and Klass8 never asks for your PIN.

Does it follow my school's syllabus?

Yes. Klass8 follows the MINESEC syllabuses and the term-by-term schemes of work, from Form 1 to Upper Sixth, for the GCE Ordinary and Advanced Levels. Technical students choose their stream for the ITC and the ATC.

Which phones does it work on?

Klass8 runs on Android phones: download it from this site. On any other phone or on a computer, open it in your browser.

Can I use it without internet?

Lessons you have opened or downloaded can be read without internet. The tutor, the marking and payments need a connection.

How accurate are the solutions and the marking?

Lessons are written for the syllabus and checked. Questions solved by the tutor are checked a second time before you see them, and marking follows an examiner's scheme. They are made by AI, so check important answers with your lessons or your teacher.

Can my parent follow my progress?

Yes. A parent can link to your account from a free parent account, or receive the weekly report you turn on.

What happens to my data?

We keep only what Klass8 needs to work, never sell it and show no advertising. You can delete your account at any time. Read the privacy policy.
