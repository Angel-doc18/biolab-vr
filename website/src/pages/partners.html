How it works

Five steps, all in the app

  1. Get your code

    Create a Klass8 account. Your promo code is on your Home, under Invite and earn.

  2. Share it

    Send it by WhatsApp or SMS from the app, read it out in class, or print it for your school.

  3. They join with it

    New students type your code when they create their account.

  4. You earn each time they pay

    Your share is added as soon as their mobile money payment goes through, and you are told in the app.

  5. Withdraw by mobile money

    Once you have 1,000 FCFA ready, ask for it in the app and Klass8 sends it to your MTN MoMo or Orange Money.

What you earn

A share of every payment, for 12 months

You earn 20% of what Klass8 receives each time someone who joined with your code pays. Fapshi, the mobile money service, keeps 3% of each payment first, so your share is worked out on the rest.

  • One month or one year: you earn on both
  • Every payment, not only the first
  • See each earning, and who it came from, in the app

Withdrawals

Your money, sent to your phone

Each earning can be withdrawn 7 days after the payment, once the refund window has passed.

  1. Ask in the app

    In Invite and earn, type the amount, at least 1,000 FCFA, and your MTN MoMo or Orange Money number.

  2. Klass8 sends it

    Usually within three working days, by mobile money, to the number you gave.

  3. You are told

    A notification in the app gives the amount and the transaction number.

The rules

Fair for everyone

Questions about your code or a withdrawal? Write to us.

Can I use my own code?

No. A code only counts for new accounts made by other people, and someone you invited cannot use your code back.

What if someone forgot to type my code?

They can still add it in the app, in Invite and earn, during their first 14 days.

For how long do I earn?

You earn on their payments for 12 months after they join. If the rules change, it only applies to people who join afterwards, and every partner is told in the app.

Why wait before I can withdraw?

Payments can be refunded within 7 days. Waiting 7 days means you are never asked to give money back.

Can my earnings be stopped?

Fake accounts, buying sign-ups or paying people to use a code are not allowed. When that happens Klass8 puts the code on hold and tells you why in the app. What you earned fairly stays yours.

Does using a code cost the new student anything?

No. They pay the usual price, and your share comes out of what Klass8 receives.
