The heart and circulationBiology, Form 4, lesson 3.1

Mammals have a double circulation: blood passes through the heart twice for each complete circuit of the body.

The pulmonary circulation carries blood to the lungs, and the systemic circulation carries it to the rest of the body.

The heart has four chambers. The right atrium receives deoxygenated blood from the body through the vena cava.

Exam tip. Say which side of the heart carries oxygenated blood.
