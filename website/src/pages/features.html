Everything you need to revise, in one app

Klass8 explains your lessons, solves questions with you, marks your work and gets you ready for the GCE, at the level of your own class.

Lessons Solve Marking Diagrams Exam practice

Lessons

Listen to every lesson

A natural voice reads each lesson and explains it the way a good teacher does, in English or in French. The words light up as they are read, so you can follow along, then test yourself with the questions at the end.

  • Every topic of your class, in the order of the school year
  • Worked examples and exam tips in every lesson
  • At least five questions after each lesson

Solve

Snap a question, see it solved

Photograph a question from your textbook, a past paper or your exercise book. The tutor works it out one line at a time and explains each line. A second pass works the question again and corrects any slip before you see the answer.

  • Printed or handwritten questions
  • Ask about any step you did not follow
  • A similar question to try on your own
{{SAY:board-en|Hear a step explained|Physics|light}}
{{SCREEN:solve}}

Marking

Answer on paper, get it marked

Write your answer the way you would in the exam, take a photo and get your marks against an examiner's mark scheme: what earned marks, what lost them and a model answer. It works for structured questions and for Paper 3 practicals.

  • Reads your handwriting, mistakes included
  • Marks every point of the scheme
  • Tells you exactly what to fix
{{SCREEN:mark}}

Diagrams

Diagrams that explain themselves

Every diagram is labelled the way the GCE expects, with ruled label lines that never cross. Tap a part and the tutor tells you what it is and what it does. Organs like the heart, the lungs and the brain also open in 3D, so you can turn them in your hand.

  • {{diagrams}} diagrams across the subjects
  • {{models}} anatomy models in 3D
  • Practicals with real readings, tables and graphs

Exam practice

Practise like the real exam

Timed Paper 1 multiple choice, Paper 2 structured questions and term tests for your class, with the explanation behind every answer. Klass8 shows the topics you find hardest, so you know what to revise next.

  • A new paper every time
  • Timed like the GCE
  • Your grade estimate as you practise
