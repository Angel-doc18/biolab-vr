Paying

How you pay

You pay inside the app, from your own phone. Payments are processed by Fapshi, and Klass8 never asks for your PIN.

  1. Choose a plan

    In the app, open Plans and choose one month or one year.

  2. Enter your number

    Choose MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money and type the number that will pay.

  3. Approve on your phone

    Confirm the request with your PIN. Klass8 opens as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Questions

Questions about paying

Read the refund policy, or contact us.

Does anything renew automatically?

No. Each payment is one request that you approve on your phone. When your days end, Klass8 asks you to choose a plan again.

When does a paid month start?

A month paid during your free month starts when the free month ends, so you lose no days. A year paid during your free month counts from the day you joined.

What if money is taken but Klass8 does not open?

Contact us within 14 days with your phone number and the payment reference shown in the app. We either open Klass8 on your account or refund the full amount to the same number within 7 working days.

Can a school pay for its students?

Yes. A school can buy a code for each student, who enters it in the app instead of paying. Read about school licences.
