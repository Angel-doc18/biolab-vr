Installing

Installed in four steps

Klass8 comes straight from this site. Android asks once for permission, as it does for any app that does not come from the Play Store.

  1. Download

    Tap Download the app and wait for the file to finish.

  2. Open the file

    Open Klass8.apk from your notifications or your Downloads folder.

  3. Allow the install

    If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager, then tap Install.

  4. Create your account

    Open Klass8, choose your class and your subjects, and start your free month.
