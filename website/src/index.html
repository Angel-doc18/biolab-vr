Every lesson from Form 1 to Upper Sixth, explained out loud.

Klass8 is the study app for secondary school students in Cameroon. It follows the MINESEC syllabus for your class, term by term. A natural voice reads every lesson to you, explains the diagrams and works through the examples, all the way to the GCE.

Watch a question solved on the board

Type any question, or open one from your lesson, and the tutor solves it on the board one step at a time. Each step is written out and explained aloud, the way a teacher does in class. Stuck on a step? Tap it to hear it again, or ask about it.

    Diagrams that teach, part by part

    Klass8 has {{diagrams}} labelled diagrams, drawn to scale. Press play and the tutor points to each part and explains what it is and what it does. Tap any part to hear just that one.

      Hold a real heart in your hands

      Six anatomy models, made from real human anatomy data: the heart, the lungs, the digestive system, the urinary system, the brain and the arm. Turn them, look inside, separate the parts, watch the heart beat, and test yourself by finding each part.

      On many Android phones you can stand the model on your desk with augmented reality, or step inside it with a Cardboard headset.

      Practise for the GCE, question by question

      Klass8 has {{questions}} practice questions, and every one tells you why the answer is right. Take timed Paper 1 practice papers, answer Paper 2 questions in your subject’s layout, and let the tutor mark your answer against the marking points. Type it, or photograph your handwriting.

      These ten questions come straight from Klass8 topics, from Form 2 to Lower Sixth.

      Your class, your syllabus, term by term

      Klass8 is organised the way your school year is: by class, term and sequence, following the MINESEC syllabus. In all, {{subjects}} subjects, {{topics}} topics, {{lessons}} lessons and {{workedExamples}} worked examples. Pick a class and a subject to see every topic.

      Parents and teachers can follow along

      For parents

      Each week the student can send a progress report to a parent on WhatsApp. The parent needs no app: the report arrives as a message, with the mastery in each subject, the time spent revising and the topics to work on next. Parents who use Klass8 can also link to their child’s account.

      For teachers

      Create a class for your subject and share its code with your students on WhatsApp. You see each student’s mastery, who has revised this week and who has done the practicals, and you can send work to the whole class at once.

      Everything in Klass8

      • Lessons for every class written for the MINESEC syllabus, each with worked examples, an exam tip and questions.
      • A natural voice that reads the notes aloud, in English or French.
      • The tutor: ask anything about your lesson, or have a question solved on the board.
      • Mark my answer: type or photograph a Paper 2 answer and see which marks you earned.
      • {{diagrams}} labelled diagrams that explain themselves.
      • 3D anatomy models, with augmented reality and Cardboard VR.
      • Practicals: experiments explained by your teacher’s voice, with a workbook for your results.
      • Paper 1 and Paper 2 practice in the GCE layout of each subject.
